# Enterprise Diagnosis Skill

A reusable skill for diagnosing a company's commercial problem, operating constraints, business model, and growth options from a client brief or available evidence.

## What it does

- Identifies what the company sells, who pays, why customers buy, and where revenue and margin come from.
- Separates verified facts, company claims, third-party evidence, customer evidence, assumptions, and inference.
- Traces symptoms to likely mechanisms and identifies evidence that can distinguish plausible causes.
- Assesses reachable customers, substitutes, sales friction, pricing and value logic, delivery capacity, and economics when data supports it.
- Recommends a short, prioritized set of actions with target behavior, business mechanism, resources, measurable results, and low-cost tests.

## Use

Place this folder in a directory scanned by your agent tool as a skill, or provide `SKILL.md` to an agent that supports skill instructions.
