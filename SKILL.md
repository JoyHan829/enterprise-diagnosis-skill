# Enterprise Diagnosis

Identify the actual business problem before recommending communications or marketing. Establish what the company sells, who pays, why they buy, where revenue and margin come from, how customers are acquired and retained, and what operational constraints limit growth.

Separate verified facts, company claims, third-party evidence, user/customer evidence, assumptions, and inference. For current company or market facts, verify with current reliable sources when available. Do not mistake visibility, engagement, or brand awareness for commercial performance.

Trace the causal chain from the stated symptom to its likely mechanism. Compare plausible causes and identify what evidence would distinguish them. Assess the reachable customer segment, substitutes, sales friction, pricing/value logic, delivery capacity, and economics when data supports it. State what is unknown rather than manufacturing precision.

Recommend a small number of actions in priority order. For each, state the target behavior, business mechanism, required resources, measurable result, and a low-cost test. Explain what should not be prioritized when it does not address the diagnosed constraint.
